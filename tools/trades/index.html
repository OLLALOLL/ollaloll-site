Hook it up to a mob

Add these components to the entity that should trade (inside its components). The mob also needs the trading behavior minecraft:behavior.trade_with_player, which is part of how villager-style mobs work.

Good to know

  • A trade takes one or two items and gives one item. For bigger prices, use the amount (up to 64) or add a second item.
  • Tiers unlock as the trader gains XP from trades. XP needed must go up from tier to tier.
  • Random few shows each trader only some of a tier’s trades, picked at random.
  • The tool checks the format, not whether an item exists. A typo makes Minecraft log an error. Custom items use their own namespace, like mypack:ruby.
  • Enchanted books and random enchantment levels aren’t covered here.

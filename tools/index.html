Tools

Free tools for Minecraft Bedrock creators. They all run in your browser — nothing you add is uploaded.

Skins and images

  • Skin Pack BuilderTurn PNG skins into a Minecraft Bedrock skin pack (.mcpack). Add as many skins as you like, preview them, and download.Open →
  • Image ConverterConvert JPEG (and WebP, GIF, BMP) images to PNG. Do many at once and download them as a zip.Open →

Addon files

  • UUID GeneratorMake a pack and module UUID pair for your manifest.json, with a ready-made manifest to copy.Open →
  • Loot Table BuilderAdd items with weights and amounts, and get a working loot table JSON for chests, mobs and more.Open →
  • Recipe BuilderClick a 3×3 grid and choose the result. Shaped, shapeless and furnace recipes.Open →
  • Trading Table BuilderMake villager trades from items, prices and trade limits, with a live preview.Open →

Good to know

Each repeater can be set to delay a signal by 1–4 redstone ticks (0.1s–0.4s). Chain repeaters to add their delays together.

A comparator always delays by 1 redstone tick (same as a repeater on its shortest setting) — its delay isn't adjustable.

For multi-second or longer delays, a long repeater chain gets bulky fast — a hopper clock (items shuttled between hoppers and read by a comparator) packs a much longer delay into a small space, at the cost of being trickier to set up.
